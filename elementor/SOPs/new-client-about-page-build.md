# SOP: New Client About Page Build

Use this SOP to build the standard About Us page during the first full New Client Build.

The About Us page is a mandatory first-run deliverable. It is not an optional follow-up page and it is separate from the paired `Why Choose Us` and `Why Trust Us` sections on the Home page.

## Goal

Create a substantial, human-facing About Us page that helps prospective clients understand the business, services, coverage and booking process while giving search engines and AI systems a clear, truthful company profile.

The page must remain an editable Elementor page built from the approved master template and must match the Home page's established design system.

## Required Inputs

- Company name
- Primary service category
- Primary location
- Active services and published service-page URLs
- Primary service regions
- Main customer types
- Approved phone, WhatsApp and other contact details
- Verified business facts, proof and credentials when supplied
- Approved client, team, vehicle, equipment and location images
- Home page hero, CTA, list, FAQ and form component references

Primary location controls the About page's local copy style. Barry must use the correct English variant, local place hierarchy, terminology, units, quoting/estimate language and customer expectations for that location. If the Primary location is missing or ambiguous, ask before writing or applying final copy.

## Approved Page Structure

### 1. Hero

- Use one clear H1 that identifies the company, service category and primary location naturally.
- Keep the supporting copy to one concise paragraph of about 40-50 words. Confirm that the visitor has found the right business and explain the next practical action.
- Use the same approved global hero form as the rest of the site when the template includes it.
- Add two short hero trust bubbles. Each bubble must:
  - use two or three words
  - remain on one line on mobile
  - use similar character length to its partner
  - be relevant to company trust, local service or planning
  - match the Home page's approved yellow background, black text and icon, white border, radius, padding and typography
- Match the approved Home hero overlay intensity and responsive alignment.

### 2. Who We Are

- Use the eyebrow `WHO WE ARE`.
- Write a descriptive H2 that includes the service category and primary location naturally.
- Use one purposeful paragraph that explains who the business helps, what it provides and how work is planned.
- Add three complete `Label: explanation` bullet points covering:
  - the main service scope
  - important secondary or supporting services
  - how location, access or site conditions affect planning
- Keep the list left-aligned on desktop and mobile unless the approved template intentionally uses another alignment. Do not centre the bullet widget.
- Use the approved square 1000x1000 inline image treatment and the standard global `Call Us Now` and `WhatsApp Us` CTAs.

### 3. Why Choose Us or Why Trust Us

- Use a client-facing H2, a purposeful H3, one concise paragraph and three complete `Label: explanation` bullets.
- Explain service fit, communication, preparation, access, equipment, scope and realistic expectations.
- Use verified proof when available. Otherwise build trust through honest service and process detail.
- Never invent ratings, guarantees, credentials, insurance, years in business, response times or completed-project counts.

### 4. Services and Company Profile

- Explain the active services in plain language and link to the corresponding published service pages.
- Make the company profile useful to customers and entity crawlers. Clearly identify the business name, service category, primary location, customer types, service regions and practical booking route.
- Do not pad the page with generic mission statements, corporate filler or claims that cannot be verified.

### 5. Service Areas

- Summarise the approved primary service regions and link only to published location pages.
- Reuse the approved unique place image for each named primary region.
- Do not research or populate suburb cards here. The full Service Areas hub remains a separate add-on.

### 6. FAQs

- Copy the approved Home page nested FAQ widget so the widget type, icon behaviour, borders, colours, typography, spacing, animation and FAQ schema remain uniform.
- Preserve the approved widget styling exactly. Update only question and answer text when the Home questions do not suit the About page.
- Use visible, useful questions about services, customers, coverage, access, information required and booking.
- Keep FAQ schema enabled and ensure the rendered schema matches the visible questions and answers.

### 7. Final CTA

- End with a clear company-and-location CTA that tells the visitor what information to provide and how to contact the business.
- Use the approved global CTA buttons with the exact labels `Call Us Now` and `WhatsApp Us`.
- Keep the CTA visually separate from the footer. It must use the approved About page background treatment, accent border and spacing rather than an uninterrupted black section that merges into a black footer.
- Preserve the approved template's tightened desktop, tablet and mobile padding. Do not replace it with generic section spacing.

## Content Rules

- Write for clients first, then optimise for search.
- Use direct, human, locally relevant language.
- Explain what the business does, who it helps, where it works, what information it needs and what the visitor should do next.
- Use the focus keyphrase and useful variations naturally in the H1, selected H2s, opening copy and metadata.
- Do not repeat the same service-and-location phrase mechanically.
- Use one paragraph per split-layout content section and let bullet lists carry supporting detail.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Do not expose AI drafting language, placeholders, unsupported superlatives or generic corporate filler.
- Keep all contact details and CTA destinations synchronized with the approved global contact source.

## Images

- Use approved client or approved generated images.
- Paired content and support-image slots must use actual 1000x1000 square source files.
- Hero and section backgrounds must use compressed 1920x1080 landscape WebP files unless the master template documents another ratio.
- Location cards must use unique images of the named location, with honest filenames and alt text.
- Do not use inherited template-client images or wrong-ratio files hidden by cropping.

## SEO and Schema

- Apply a unique focus keyphrase, SEO title, meta description and canonical URL through Yoast.
- Target green Yoast results where practical, but keep copy truthful and readable.
- Link to active service pages, the Services hub, Service Areas hub where published, and the contact section.
- Validate the rendered page title, meta description, canonical, social metadata and FAQ schema.
- Keep the sitewide Organization or LocalBusiness entity consistent with the client schema plan. Do not create a conflicting second business entity.

## Elementor Workflow

1. Confirm the approved imported About page is an Elementor record.
2. Export the page before structural or high-risk styling changes.
3. Read the current About and Home page structures once and save the element map.
4. Reuse the approved Home hero bubbles, global form, global CTAs, list treatment and nested FAQ widget.
5. Replace inherited copy, images, links, contact details and metadata through Elementor MCP.
6. Preserve the approved section order, spacing system, responsive alignment and Elementor editability.
7. Label changed or added elements using Barry's Elementor labelling standard.
8. Verify stored settings, rendered output and mobile alignment.

## Completion Criteria

The About Us page is complete only when:

- it is included in the first full New Client Build
- the H1 and opening paragraph clearly identify the company, service category and location
- the hero contains the approved two trust bubbles and global form where applicable
- `Who We Are` contains one paragraph and three useful bullets
- services, customers and primary service regions are explained accurately
- service and location links resolve to published pages or approved destinations
- the exact approved Home FAQ widget treatment is used and FAQ schema matches visible content
- the final CTA is visually separate from the footer
- global CTA labels and destinations are correct
- Yoast fields are populated and rendered metadata is verified
- no inherited-client residue, placeholders, em dashes or unsupported claims remain
- desktop and mobile spacing match the approved About page standard
