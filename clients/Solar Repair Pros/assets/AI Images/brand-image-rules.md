# Solar Repair Pros Brand Image Rules

Approved style reference: `solar-repair-pros-approved-style-01.png`

## Visual Direction

- Photorealistic, natural commercial photography.
- Realistic solar repair, diagnostic, maintenance, electrical upgrade, battery and EV-charger work.
- Dallas-Fort Worth residential and light-commercial context where visible.
- Clean navy workwear with restrained orange safety accents.
- Natural daylight, believable tools, accurate PPE and practical work scenes.
- Calm, competent and useful rather than dramatic or staged.

## Hero Composition

- Place the technician on the left side, close to the center.
- Keep the right side clear for the contact form.
- Use a wide landscape composition suitable for a full-width background.
- Keep faces, hands, panels, tools and safety equipment realistic.

## Brand Safety

- Do not place the Solar Repair Pros logo or any other logo on uniforms, caps, tools, vehicles or equipment.
- Do not generate readable company names or branded text because the logo may change.
- Do not use sparks, flames, fake electricity, storm drama or exaggerated failure scenes.
- Avoid dark-theme imagery, heavy black backgrounds and oversaturated orange grading.
- Do not imply certifications, licenses, ratings, warranties or manufacturer authorization visually or in captions.
