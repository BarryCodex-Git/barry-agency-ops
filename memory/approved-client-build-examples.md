## Northstar Visual Contract

- Global palette is the vocabulary; Elementor element assignments determine the final readable result.
- `Brand Primary`: orange for selected emphasis, icons, active FAQ state and primary CTA treatment.
- `Brand Dark`: near-black for secondary CTA, form/cards and image overlays.
- `Text On Dark`: white.
- `Body Text`: black/near-black on white or light surfaces.
- Heroes and image-overlay sections: explicit near-black overlay with white essential text.
- Forms: near-black surface, white heading/text where applicable, white neutral border, white fields and a contrasting orange action.
- FAQ: white normal state, near-black hover with white text, orange active state with dark text, orange border treatment.
- Icons: foreground and circle/background must be checked as a pair; neither may disappear into the other.
- No unexplained old-template blue, orange-on-orange, dark-on-dark, white-on-white or blank CTA state is acceptable.

## Northstar Content Contract

- H1 contains the focus service/entity and primary location naturally.
- H2s distribute close service/location variants across distinct customer decisions.
- Headings must tell a visitor what problem, service, choice or question the section addresses.
- Introductions explain customer value rather than restating a heading or narrating the writing process.
- Each service page is original even when visual sections repeat.
- The current client-provided Service Page Template determines which sections are populated.
- The human-facing copy gate and SEO heading gate must pass before copy is applied to Elementor.

## What Not To Reuse

- Do not copy Northstar wording into another client build.
- Do not assume Northstar's six-section service template is universal.
- Do not assume orange, black and white fit another logo.
- Do not use this corrected result to excuse the failed first pass. The reusable lesson is the early gate and semantic-role workflow that prevents those defects.
