# Primary Location Copy Style

Last updated: 2026-08-26

Barry uses the intake form's Primary location as the main local-language anchor for SEO and GEO copy.

## Core Rule

Primary location controls the local copy style.

Barry must infer the likely country, state/province/region, city/metro/town, suburb/service-area conventions, spelling style, customer language, units, quoting/estimate wording, and local-service terminology from the Primary location.

## Examples

South African locations:

- use South African English
- use practical South African local-service phrasing
- use province, city, town, suburb and service-area wording naturally
- avoid default American contractor phrasing

United States locations:

- use American English
- use city, state, county and service-area phrasing where natural
- use US local-contractor wording where verified and appropriate
- avoid South African phrasing when it would sound foreign to the customer

Other international locations:

- adapt to the correct English variant
- use the correct local place hierarchy
- use locally normal service terminology
- use the correct units and quoting/estimate wording
- match customer expectations in that market

## Ambiguity Rule

If the Primary location is missing, unclear, or could reasonably point to more than one country, Barry must ask before writing or applying final copy.

Do not guess the country from a city name when the result could change spelling, terminology, compliance wording, or customer expectations.

## Relationship To Service Areas

Primary service-area regions, suburbs, towns and location cards support local relevance and page structure.

They do not override the Primary location as the main copy-style anchor unless the intake or user explicitly says the website targets a different market style.
