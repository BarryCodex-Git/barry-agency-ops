WARNING SIGNS

Signs Your Drain Problem Needs Proper Attention

Blocked drains rarely fix themselves for long. If the same sink, toilet, shower or outside gully keeps giving trouble, there is usually a build-up, obstruction or pipe condition that needs a proper look.

Slow Drainage

Water that drains slowly from a sink, shower, bath or outside gully can point to grease, soap build-up, sand, roots or a narrowing in the line.

Bad Smells

Sewer smells around bathrooms, kitchens or outdoor drains often mean waste is sitting in the pipe instead of moving away properly.

Gurgling Pipes

Gurgling after flushing or draining can show that air is struggling to move through the system because the line is partly blocked.

Repeat Blockages

A drain that clears and blocks again needs more than another quick attempt. The cause may sit deeper in the line.

BLOCKED DRAIN FAQS

Blocked Drain Questions People Ask Before Booking

Useful answers for Garden Route homeowners and businesses dealing with slow drains, smells, outside gullies, repeat blockages and blocked toilets.

Why does my drain keep blocking after I clear it?

A drain that blocks again usually has a cause sitting deeper than the visible trap or toilet pan. Grease, soap residue, roots, debris, poor pipe fall or a damaged section can all create repeat trouble. Clearing the immediate blockage helps, but the pattern matters because it tells the plumber whether more investigation may be needed.

Can H2O Plumbers help if an outside gully is overflowing?

Yes. An overflowing outside gully can mean the branch drain or main property line is not moving waste away properly. H2O Plumbers can check the affected area, clear the accessible blockage where possible and advise if the symptoms suggest a deeper line issue or possible municipal-side problem.

Should I use drain chemicals before calling a plumber?

Repeated chemical use is not ideal. It can be harsh, may not reach the real obstruction and can make the work area less pleasant or safe to handle. If a drain is slow, smells bad or keeps backing up, a practical plumbing check is usually better than adding more chemicals.
